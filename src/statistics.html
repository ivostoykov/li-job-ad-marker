How counting works
  • A card counts when at least half of it is visible while marking is on.
  • Reloading, opening another results page or running a new search counts the cards again.
  • Scrolling back, selecting a job or LinkedIn redrawing the list does not.
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Days follow this computer's local time.
  • Private windows are not counted. Statistics stay on this device: they are not synced or included in backups, and importing a backup leaves them unchanged.
